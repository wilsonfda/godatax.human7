# GoDataX Human 7ph — References and Content Studied

> Summary of where and how each reference in the bibliography of the scientific
> article `GoDataX_Human7_Artigo_Cientifico.docx` (and its English version,
> `Andrade_2026_GoDataX_Human7ph_Metaphysicometrics_EN.docx`) is actually used
> in the text. This is not a summary of the original sources themselves — it
> is what the article drew from / cited from each one.

| # | Reference | Section(s) Used | Content Studied |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AERA, APA, NCME (2014). *Standards for Educational and Psychological Testing.* | Section 5 | The reference standard for what counts as validity evidence for a test. Used to list what is still missing before the OT7 prototype can be considered validated. |
| 2 | Andrade, W. F. D. et al. *Instrumentos de Medida em Contexto Militar* (manuscript in preparation). | Sections 3, 4 | The source of the dataset itself (n = 423 cadets, Military Academy, 2019) reanalyzed in the article — the 20 psychometric scores and the original CAAS factor structure come from this study. |
| 3 | Thomas Aquinas. *Summa Theologiae*, I, Q. 2, Art. 3. | Table 1; Section 2.3; Section 5 | The Five Ways (motion, efficient cause, contingency, degrees of perfection, governance/finality) — the philosophical foundation of the entire metaphysical layer, with the Fifth Way anchoring the OT7 construct. |
| 4 | COSMIN, *COnsensus-based Standards for the selection of health Measurement Instruments.* | Section 5 | Methodological guidance on content validity (relevance, comprehensiveness, comprehensibility) of an instrument. Used together with [1] as a criterion for what is missing to validate the OT7. |
| 5 | Casarotto, E. Instituto Virtudes, *Virtology*. Institutional material. | Section 2.6; Tables 8, 9 | The full Virtology framework — the Pride System / Selfishness System and the catalogue of 33 trainable virtues — underlying the score↔virtue correspondence tables. |
| 6 | GoDataX. *GoDataX Human 7.* GoDataX.com. | Introduction | Self-citation of the umbrella GoDataX Human 7 project/website, of which this article (GDX H7ph) is the scientific excerpt. |
| 7 | Moshontz, H., Ebersole, C. R., Weston, S. J., & Klein, R. A. (2021). *Investigating psychological phenomena through collaborative approaches.* | Section 5, Step 3 | The three-component model of persistence in episodic goal pursuit (resisting abandonment, recognizing opportunities, returning to the pursuit). Underlies OT7's "adaptive persistence" behavioral link. |
| 8 | Nova Acrópole (2023). *A Constituição Septenária do Ser Humano.* Philosophical study material, Lisbon. | Section 2.4; Tables 2, 5, 6 | The full seven-level/body model (Physical → Atmic/Spirit), studied in person by the author at this school (Lisbon, 2023) — the basis for every table that crosses a psychometric score with a septenary level. |
| 9 | Ryan, R. M., & Frederick, C. (1997). *On energy, personality, and health: subjective vitality as a dynamic reflection of well-being.* | Section 5, Step 1 | The construct of "subjective vitality" as a self-reported psychological variable, distinct from "metaphysical energy." Used to keep vitality as a separate, non-metaphysical observable in the OT7 prototype. |
