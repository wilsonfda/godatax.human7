Psychometric Instruments: Response Scores vs. Factor-Analysis Loadings

Pure-psychometric perspective (cardiac coherence deliberately excluded): one instrument per x-axis position, two panels on separate y-scales. The top panel's RAW_SCORE (mostly 1-5 Likert, but not a uniform scale across instruments -- e.g. Positivity Ratio Scale runs 0-4) is a fundamentally different quantity from the bottom panel's factor loadings (-1 to 1, from GDX_PSY_PSYCHOMETRIC_PROPERTY), so each gets its own panel rather than one shared axis (the dual-axis anti-pattern) or an arbitrary indexed baseline (there is no natural "instrument zero" the way chart 1 has a BASELINE phase). None of this study's instrument(s) has any EFA/CFA row yet -- every bottom-panel point is absent, not zero.
