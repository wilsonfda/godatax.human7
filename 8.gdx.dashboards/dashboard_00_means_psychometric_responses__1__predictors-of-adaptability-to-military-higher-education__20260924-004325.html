Psychometric Instruments: Response Scores vs. Factor-Analysis Loadings

Pure-psychometric perspective (cardiac coherence deliberately excluded): one instrument per x-axis position, two panels on separate y-scales. The top panel's RAW_SCORE (mostly 1-5 Likert, but not a uniform scale across instruments -- e.g. Positivity Ratio Scale runs 0-4) is a fundamentally different quantity from the bottom panel's factor loadings (-1 to 1, from GDX_PSY_PSYCHOMETRIC_PROPERTY), so each gets its own panel rather than one shared axis (the dual-axis anti-pattern) or an arbitrary indexed baseline (there is no natural "instrument zero" the way chart 1 has a BASELINE phase). Only Hardiness, Student Engagement Scale, Career Adapt-Abilities Scale, Satisfaction With Life Scale, Job Satisfaction Scale, Positivity Ratio Scale, Maslach Burnout Inventory - Student Survey (hypothesis - unconfirmed), Undocumented Organizational Block (hypothesis - an1-an11), Red Tape / Perceived Bureaucracy (hypothesis - red1-red5) has EFA/CFA rows among this study's instruments -- every other bottom-panel point here is absent, not zero.
